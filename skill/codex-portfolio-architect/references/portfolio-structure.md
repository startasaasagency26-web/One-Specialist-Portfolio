# Company Portfolio Structure

## Recommended single-page structure

### 1. Hero
Purpose: establish value, tone, and authority immediately.

Include:
- sharp headline
- supporting line
- primary CTA
- premium media or 3d moment
- optional stat or trust cue

### 2. Brand or Company Intro
Purpose: explain who the company is in plain language.

Include:
- short company statement
- what makes it different
- concise visual support

### 3. Services or Capabilities
Purpose: show what the company actually does.

Include:
- 3 to 6 service blocks
- business outcome framing
- optional process preview

### 4. Showcase or Proof
Purpose: show evidence of competence.

Possible forms:
- portfolio items
- before/after examples
- client categories
- internal process visuals
- metrics only if verified

### 5. Process
Purpose: reduce friction and build confidence.

Include:
- simple step sequence
- what the client can expect
- speed, communication, or quality differentiators

### 6. Trust Layer
Purpose: answer unspoken objections.

Possible forms:
- testimonials
- guarantees
- certifications
- FAQs
- operating principles

### 7. Closing CTA
Purpose: convert attention into action.

Include:
- specific CTA
- contact route
- reassurance sentence

## Multipage escalation

Split into multiple pages when:
- services need real depth
- portfolio/work has enough material
- SEO page targets matter
- the company has distinct audiences or verticals

Keep Home as the narrative sales page even in a multipage site.

3. Speed (ship fast, don't over-engineer)
4. Repeatability (scalable patterns, documented structure)
5. Delegation (code any staff member can maintain)

7. Aesthetics (last — beauty serves positioning, not the other way)

## Design Standard

Unless Nick explicitly overrides:
- Apple-style restraint: restrained, not flashy
- Large typography, strong hierarchy
- Heavy whitespace, clear alignment
- Cinematic section pacing
- One premium hero moment (3d or motion)
- One secondary depth moment maximum
- DOM/CSS for everything that does not need WebGL
- No carousels, random blobs, or decorative motion with no narrative purpose

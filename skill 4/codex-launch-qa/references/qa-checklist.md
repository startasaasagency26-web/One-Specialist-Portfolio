# QA Checklist

## Structure
- navigation works
- sections appear in correct order
- no dead routes or placeholder links

## Responsive
- hero survives small screens
- headings do not collapse awkwardly
- sticky sections do not trap users
- CTA remains visible and usable

## Motion
- no jank during scroll
- reduced-motion mode works
- timing feels intentional, not delayed for drama
- motion does not block comprehension

## 3D
- canvas is responsive
- fallback appears when needed
- scene does not crush mobile performance
- object motion is restrained

## Content
- no lorem ipsum
- no duplicated copy
- service labels are clear
- trust claims are verified
- CTA is specific

## Documentation
- setup steps are complete
- dev and build commands are present
- deployment steps are explicit
- animation notes identify which library powers what

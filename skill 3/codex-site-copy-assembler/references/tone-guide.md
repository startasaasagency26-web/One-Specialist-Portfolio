# Tone Guide

## What the copy should feel like

- premium
- precise
- capable
- calm
- modern

## What it should not feel like

- desperate
- noisy
- overhyped
- jargon-heavy
- robotic luxury cosplay

## Editing checklist

Cut or rewrite lines that:
- could apply to any company
- rely on empty adjectives
- repeat the same idea in different words
- sound like a brochure from 2017
- make claims the user did not provide

## Preferred sentence behavior

- short opening lines
- clear nouns and verbs
- one idea per sentence when possible
- occasional contrast for emphasis

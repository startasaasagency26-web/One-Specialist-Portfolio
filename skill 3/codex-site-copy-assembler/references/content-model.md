# Content Model

## Hero
Need:
- 3 to 5 headline options
- 2 to 3 supporting lines
- 1 primary CTA
- optional eyebrow

## About
Need:
- one concise company statement
- short paragraph or bullet group
- optional principles list

## Services
Need:
- service titles
- short descriptors
- optional extended lines for cards or detail pages

## Proof
Need one of:
- project summaries
- operating strengths
- verified metrics
- testimonial snippets

## Process
Need:
- step names
- one-line explanation per step
- optional reassurance line

## FAQ / Trust
Need:
- objection-focused questions
- direct, clean answers

## CTA
Need:
- closing headline
- reassurance sentence
- specific call to action
